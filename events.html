THE DEFINITIVE HOLI

A Festival Without Equal

Acclaimed as one of the most sophisticated Holi celebrations in India, our gathering is reserved exclusively for international residents and invited guests. A safe, vibrant, and traditional environment where organic pigments and folk rhythms define the day.

THE FESTIVAL OF LIGHTS

Diwali, In Its Truest Royal Form

Thousands of oil lamps trace the courtyards, block-printing artists work live beneath the arches, and a royal Rajasthani dinner welcomes guests from around the world — an evening that lingers long after the diyas are lit.

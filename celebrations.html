Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Diggi Palace a good wedding venue in Jaipur? expand_more

Yes. Diggi Palace is one of Jaipur's most celebrated heritage wedding venues, offering three unique spaces: Durbar Hall (500 guests), Green Sanctuary garden (300 guests) and the intimate Zenana Courtyard (80 guests), set in a 1860 ancestral palace.

What is the capacity for weddings at Diggi Palace? expand_more

Diggi Palace can host weddings from 80 to 500 guests across its three venues. The Durbar Hall suits grand receptions, the Green Sanctuary garden weddings, and the Zenana Courtyard intimate ceremonies.

Does Diggi Palace offer destination wedding packages? expand_more

Yes. The Diggi Palace events team curates complete destination wedding packages including accommodation, catering, décor and cultural entertainment for both Indian and international couples.

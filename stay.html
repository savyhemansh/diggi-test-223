Stay at Diggi Palace

Step through the gates and the city simply… stops. The horns, the heat, the crush of the old town — gone, swallowed by lawns and fruit trees and the slow shadow of a haveli that has stood since 1860. Peacocks move across the grass like they own it, because in a sense, they do. This is not a hotel that borrowed a palace. This is the palace — still home to the family who built it, still breathing, still impossibly, almost unfairly quiet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What rooms are available at Diggi Palace? expand_more

Diggi Palace offers four categories: Palace Suite (most luxurious, with vaulted ceilings and butler service), Courtyard Suite (private garden courtyard), Heritage Deluxe (hand-painted ceilings, garden views) and Heritage Standard (classic heritage comfort).

What is the check-in and check-out time at Diggi Palace? expand_more

Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are available on request subject to availability.

Is Diggi Palace pet friendly? expand_more

Yes, Diggi Palace is a pet-friendly heritage hotel in Jaipur. Guests may bring pets subject to hotel policy — please contact the hotel in advance.

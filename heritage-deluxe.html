Heritage Deluxe

A Journey Through Time

Welcome to the Heritage Deluxe room, where the allure of the past seamlessly combines with modern comforts to create an enchanting stay in Jaipur. Stepping into our Heritage Deluxe Rooms is akin to embarking on a journey through time.

The elegant decor and thoughtful design pay homage to the rich history and culture of the region. Nestled in the heart of the Pink City, this iconic palace-style property showcases elegant architecture, beautifully appointed rooms, and warm Rajasthani hospitality. Guests can enjoy refined dining experiences, serene courtyards, and luxurious amenities that reflect Jaipur’s rich cultural legacy.

As a restored heritage palace, no two rooms are ever the same. Each space carries its own architectural character, original detailing, proportions, and timeless charm — thoughtfully preserved to offer an experience that feels deeply personal, authentic, and unmistakably luxurious.

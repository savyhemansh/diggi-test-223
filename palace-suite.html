Palace Suite

A Sovereign Sanctuary

Centuries of royal history breathe through the walls of the Palace Suite. Each sanctuary offers a seamless blend of heritage craftsmanship and modern repose. Towering arched windows look out upon the inner courtyards of Diggi Palace, where peacocks roam freely amidst the bougainvillea.

The Royal Living Space is defined by its architectural grandeur, featuring vaulted ceilings and bespoke furniture crafted from reclaimed teak. Hand-woven silk rugs from Jaipur cover the polished sandstone floors, while curated art pieces from the Royal family's private collection adorn the walls.

Inside, the Palatial Bedroom provides a haven of absolute seclusion. A king-sized bed is accompanied by a sun-drenched reading nook, perfect for slow mornings. The master ensuite is a marvel of craftsmanship, featuring a deep soaking tub carved from a single block of Udaipur green marble and a separate dressing chamber fit for royalty.

As a restored heritage palace, no two rooms are ever the same. Each space carries its own architectural character, original detailing, proportions, and timeless charm — thoughtfully preserved to offer an experience that feels deeply personal, authentic, and unmistakably luxurious.

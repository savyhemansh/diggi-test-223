THE GENESIS

The Vision of Shri Thakur Saheb Pratap Singh Ji

Established in the mid-19th century, Diggi Palace was conceived not just as a royal residence but as a sanctuary for art, culture, and statesmanship. The architectural language reflects a seamless blend of Rajput heritage and functional elegance.

"To preserve is to honor the ancestors; to evolve is to serve the future."

A CHRONOLOGY OF ELEGANCE

The Diggi Era

1860

Foundations Laid

The core structure of the palace was completed, serving as the primary seat of the Diggi family — a beacon of power and architectural grace in the heart of Jaipur.

1991

A Gracious Welcome

The family opened their doors to the world, transforming the private estate into an ultra-luxury heritage hotel while fiercely protecting its historical soul.

Today

Living Heritage

Today, Diggi Palace stands as a beacon of Rajasthani hospitality — a living testament to timeless tradition, blending ancient elegance with modern luxury.

Best Time to Visit Jaipur: A Month-by-Month Guide

22 January 2026 · The Diggi Journal

Jaipur sits in a part of Rajasthan that swings between two extremes — cool, pleasant winters and genuinely fierce summer heat — which makes timing your visit more important here than in most Indian cities.

October to March: peak season

This is when most visitors come, and for good reason. Daytime temperatures sit in a comfortable range for walking around forts and bazaars, evenings are cool enough for rooftop dinners, and the city's major festivals — including the Jaipur Literature Festival in January — fall in this window. Expect higher hotel rates and busier monuments, especially around Diwali and JLF week.

April to June: peak summer heat

Temperatures regularly climb past 40°C, and midday sightseeing becomes genuinely uncomfortable. This is the season for early-morning monument visits and long, shaded afternoons — many heritage properties, including palace courtyards with mature trees and water features, are noticeably more bearable than the open city streets. Room rates drop significantly, which suits travellers prioritising value over comfort.

July to September: monsoon

Rajasthan's monsoon is lighter than coastal India's, but it brings welcome relief from the heat, dramatic skies, and a green tint to the usually dusty countryside around Jaipur. Rainfall is intermittent rather than constant, so it's a workable — and quiet — time to visit if you don't mind occasional showers.

Festival timing to plan around

Teej (monsoon, July/August) and Gangaur (spring, March/April) fill Jaipur's streets with processions and colour; Diwali transforms the old city into a city of lights each October or November; and the Jaipur Literature Festival, held every January, draws readers and writers from across the world to venues including Diggi Palace's historic grounds.

Our recommendation

For a first visit, October through February offers the best balance of comfortable weather and full access to festivals and sightseeing. If you've been before and want a quieter, better-value trip, late June through September rewards travellers willing to work around the heat or rain.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best month to visit Jaipur? expand_more

October through February offers the most comfortable weather for sightseeing in Jaipur, combined with major festivals like Diwali and the January Jaipur Literature Festival.

Is Jaipur too hot to visit in summer? expand_more

April to June brings temperatures regularly above 40°C, making midday sightseeing difficult, though early mornings and shaded heritage properties remain manageable, and hotel rates are significantly lower.

Does Jaipur get monsoon rain? expand_more

Yes, from roughly July to September, though rainfall tends to be lighter and more intermittent than in coastal parts of India.

Frequently Asked Questions

What restaurants are inside Diggi Palace Jaipur? expand_more

Diggi Palace has two restaurants: Baradari, serving royal Rajasthani thali and fine dining under historic arches; and La Brass, an all-day bistro offering global and Indian cuisine throughout the day.

Is Rajasthani food available at Diggi Palace? expand_more

Yes. Baradari Restaurant at Diggi Palace is renowned for its authentic Rajasthani royal thali — laal maas, dal baati churma, gatte ki sabzi and seasonal specialities served in a heritage dining setting.

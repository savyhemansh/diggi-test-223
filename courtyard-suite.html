Courtyard Suite

A Haven of Tranquillity

Welcome to the Courtyard Suite at Diggi Palace, a sanctuary where historical grandeur meets contemporary refinement. This suite is designed for those who seek the quiet luxury of Rajasthan’s heritage.

Each suite opens onto a private courtyard framed by original palace walls, offering an exclusive outdoor sanctuary. Inside, the architecture pays homage to local craftsmanship with high vaulted ceilings, intricate stonework, and a layout that invites the natural flow of light and air. The separate living area, finished in sand-toned marbles and fine textiles, provides an elegant space for relaxation or intimate hosting.

As a restored heritage palace, no two rooms are ever the same. Each space carries its own architectural character, original detailing, proportions, and timeless charm — thoughtfully preserved to offer an experience that feels deeply personal, authentic, and unmistakably luxurious.

CURATED EXPERIENCES

Extraordinary Moments

Terrace Baithak

As twilight settles across Diggi Palace, the terrace awakens beneath lantern glow and drifting melodies. Low Rajasthani seating, brass trays of fresh mithai and masala chai, and musicians whose instruments have echoed through Rajasthan for centuries create an evening that feels less arranged and more discovered.

The Oasis Lunch

Hidden within the quieter corners of the estate, a private luncheon appears beneath ancient trees and filtered sunlight. Crystal stemware catches the afternoon glow while regional delicacies arrive in unhurried courses. Time moves differently here. Conversations linger.

The Kitchen Table

From our own farm to a wood-fired tawa, guests roll dough, temper spices, and plate a Rajasthani thali under the guidance of our kitchen elders. Seasonal art workshops run alongside — brush in hand, painting Jarocha windows and palace motifs on quiet afternoons.

Beyond The Palace

Diggi Bagh, The Family's Countryside Estate

A second home outside Jaipur, held by the same family for generations — open fields, working farmland, and a slower rhythm.

Beyond The Palace · Diggi Bagh

Camel Safari & Countryside Mornings

At Diggi Bagh, the family's countryside estate outside Jaipur, mornings open onto open fields rather than courtyards — a camel safari at golden hour, yoga beneath the sky, and organic produce picked from the estate's own farm.

Beyond The Palace · Diggi Bagh

Organic Farming & Heritage Cooking

Walk the working farm, harvest what's in season, then cook it alongside the Diggi Bagh kitchen using recipes passed down through the family — followed by a guided history walk across the estate.

Tailoring with Artisans

Step inside the quiet rhythm of the karkhana. Work alongside master craftsmen whose hands carry generations of memory — block printing, mirror-work, and the slow embroidery that turns cloth into heirloom.

02 — The Companion

Pet-Friendly Retreats

A welcoming sanctuary for your loyal companions — bespoke amenities, open lawns, and quiet courtyards where they roam at ease.

03 — The Stillness

Wellness & Serenity

04 — The Harvest

Visit to the Organic Farms

Walk the estate's organic gardens at dawn, where heirloom vegetables and fragrant herbs are tended by hand — tracing the quiet journey from soil to royal table.

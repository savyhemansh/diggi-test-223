The Journey to Diggi

flight_takeoff

By Air

Jaipur International Airport (JAI) is approximately 12km away. Major hubs like Delhi and Mumbai offer daily connections with a flight time of 1 hour.

train

By Rail

Jaipur Junction is just 4km from the palace. Experience the luxury of the Shatabdi Express from Delhi, arriving in just under 4.5 hours.

directions_car

By Road

A scenic 5-hour drive from New Delhi via the NH48. Agra is roughly 4 hours away through the golden triangle route.

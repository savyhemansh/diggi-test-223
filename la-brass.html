MODERN SOPHISTICATION

Modern Sophistication Meets Rajasthani Heritage

La Brass Restaurant cum Bar at Diggi Palace in Jaipur is a culinary haven that seamlessly combines elegance with a vibrant ambiance. Nestled within the historical confines of Diggi Palace, this establishment offers a unique dining experience where modern sophistication meets Rajasthani heritage.

The restaurant boasts a diverse menu that celebrates the rich flavours of Rajasthan while incorporating global influences, resulting in a delightful fusion of tastes. Whether indulging in traditional Rajasthani delicacies or savouring international cuisines, patrons are treated to a feast for the senses amidst the regal splendour of Jaipur's past and contemporary allure.

GASTRONOMIC ODYSSEY

Signature Creations

Embark on a culinary odyssey at La Brass Jaipur by savouring our signature dishes. From the fiery indulgence of Laal Maas to the international comfort of Artisanal Pizzas, each plate is a testament to our diverse palate.

Indulge in succulent Grilled Chicken with Saute Vegetables, the rich flavours of Chicken Tikka Masala, the creamy perfection of Paneer Lababdar, or the aromatic allure of Thai Curry. Our well-appointed bar complements these offerings with an impressive array of craft cocktails, fine wines, and spirits.

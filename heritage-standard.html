Heritage Standard

Timeless Charm

Welcome to the Heritage Standard rooms at Diggi Palace, Jaipur, where timeless charm meets modern comfort. Nestled within the historical walls of our hotel, the Heritage Standard Room offers a delightful blend of heritage-inspired decor and contemporary amenities, promising a memorable stay that celebrates the essence of the region's cultural heritage.

Step into a sanctuary defined by historical integrity and quiet luxury. These rooms serve as a masterclass in desert minimalism—where traditional Rajasthani architectural motifs meet the refined, uncluttered philosophy of modern luxury. Every texture, from the cool limestone underfoot to the hand-woven cotton textures, is chosen to evoke a deep sense of place and profound serenity.

As a restored heritage palace, no two rooms are ever the same. Each space carries its own architectural character, original detailing, proportions, and timeless charm — thoughtfully preserved to offer an experience that feels deeply personal, authentic, and unmistakably luxurious.

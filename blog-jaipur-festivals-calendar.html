Jaipur's Festivals Calendar: Teej, Gangaur, Diwali & JLF

26 February 2026 · The Diggi Journal

Jaipur's calendar is built around a rhythm of festivals that shape the city's streets, markets and social life as much as any monument does. Timing a visit around one of them changes the character of the trip entirely.

January: Jaipur Literature Festival

JLF has grown into one of the largest free literary festivals in the world since its founding in 2006, drawing authors and readers from across the globe for five days of sessions, discussions and performances — with early editions held in the courtyards of Diggi Palace, which remained the festival's home ground for many years.

March–April: Gangaur

Gangaur celebrates the goddess Gauri and is one of Rajasthan's most important festivals for women, marked by processions through Jaipur's old city with decorated idols carried through the streets, accompanied by music and traditional dress.

July–August: Teej

Teej marks the arrival of the monsoon and is celebrated with swings hung from trees, women in vivid green traditional dress, and processions honouring the goddess Parvati — Jaipur's Teej procession is one of the city's most photographed annual events.

October–November: Diwali

Diwali transforms Jaipur's old city into a city of lights, with markets like Johari Bazaar and Bapu Bazaar strung with lamps and decorations for weeks around the festival. It's also one of the busiest and most atmospheric times to be in the city, though hotel rates and crowds both rise accordingly.

Planning a festival-timed visit

If a specific festival matters to your trip, book well in advance — dates shift each year with the lunar calendar (except JLF, which is fixed in January), and Jaipur's heritage properties fill quickly around Diwali and JLF week in particular.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Jaipur Literature Festival held? expand_more

The Jaipur Literature Festival is held every January in Jaipur, and has been running since its founding in 2006, with early editions hosted in the courtyards of Diggi Palace.

What is Gangaur? expand_more

Gangaur is a major Rajasthani festival held in March or April, celebrating the goddess Gauri with processions of decorated idols through Jaipur's old city, and is especially significant for women.

Is Diwali a good time to visit Jaipur? expand_more

Yes — Diwali (October or November) transforms Jaipur's old city markets into a city of lights, though it's also one of the busiest and most expensive times to visit, so advance booking is recommended.

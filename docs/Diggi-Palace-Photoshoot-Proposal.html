Why Professional Photography Matters

For a heritage property like Diggi Palace, the website is often the first impression a guest receives — before they ever walk through the gates. Research shows:

  • Guests spend 60% of their browsing time looking at photos before reading a single word of text. The images decide whether they stay on the website or leave.
  • Properties with professional photography receive 2–3x more direct bookings compared to those using stock or amateur images.
  • Original photography builds trust. Travellers can spot stock photos instantly — and it signals that the property isn’t confident enough to show itself. Diggi Palace deserves better.
  • Cinematic video on a hotel website increases time-on-page by 80% and creates emotional connection that photos alone cannot.

The goal: Every image on the Diggi Palace website will be an original, professionally shot and edited photograph of the actual property — its rooms, its restaurants, its gardens, its heritage. No more placeholders. No more borrowed imagery. This is Diggi Palace, as it truly is.

Professional Equipment

To do justice to a heritage property like Diggi Palace, we use professional-grade equipment rented specifically for this project:

Camera

Sony A7 IV — 33 megapixel full-frame professional camera. Exceptional detail, colour accuracy, and low-light performance. The same camera system used by architectural and hotel photographers worldwide. Shoots both high-resolution photos and cinematic 4K video.

Lenses

Wide-Angle Zoom (15–35mm f/2.8) — Captures rooms, courtyards, and architecture in their full grandeur. Makes spaces look spacious, inviting, and true to life. Essential for interior and architectural photography.

Portrait Lens (35mm f/1.4) — Creates beautiful background blur for food close-ups, detail shots, textures, and lifestyle moments. Performs exceptionally in low light — perfect for palace corridors and atmospheric restaurant interiors.

Support

Professional Tripod — For razor-sharp room interiors, long-exposure evening shots, and steady architectural frames.

Gimbal Stabiliser — For smooth, cinematic video movement — walkthrough shots, tracking shots through corridors and gardens.

Post-Production & Editing

The shoot is only half the work. Professional post-production is what separates a good photo from a stunning one. Every single image and video goes through a detailed editing process:

Photo Editing — Adobe Lightroom Classic

  • Colour grading — Consistent, warm, heritage tone across all images that matches the Diggi Palace brand
  • Exposure & white balance correction — Every photo properly exposed, natural colours even in mixed lighting
  • Lens correction — Straight vertical lines in architecture, no wide-angle distortion
  • Cropping & composition — Each image framed for maximum impact on the website
  • Detail enhancement — Sharpening textures, fabrics, architectural details
  • Blemish removal — Minor distractions, wires, or imperfections cleaned up
  • Web optimisation — Exported in multiple sizes for fast website loading without losing quality

Video Editing — DaVinci Resolve

  • Cinematic colour grading — Matching the warm, heritage look of the photo series
  • Smooth transitions — Professional cuts and fades between scenes
  • Background music — Royalty-free, elegant music that fits the palace atmosphere
  • Speed ramping — Slow-motion for dramatic moments, smooth speed for walkthroughs
  • Export for web — Compressed for fast loading, optimised for autoplay on the website

Estimated editing time: 5–7 working days. This is where the majority of post-shoot effort goes — 200+ photos, each individually processed.

Detailed 2-Day Shoot Plan

Two full days, sunrise to sunset. Every section of the property is covered in a structured schedule to make the most of natural light and palace activity.

Palace facade in warm morning light, entrance gate, garden paths, courtyard from elevated angle. The soft golden light of early morning creates the most dramatic, inviting images of the property exterior.

Heritage Standard, Heritage Deluxe, Courtyard Suite, Palace Suite. Morning window light is ideal for interiors. Wide-angle shots of full room, detail shots of bed, bathroom, view from window, amenities, textures. 7–9 final images per room type.

What We Need from the Property

To ensure the highest quality results in just 2 days, we request the following preparations before and during the shoot:

Room Preparation

  • All 4 room types made available and unoccupied during the morning of Day 1
  • Beds freshly made with crisp, ironed linens — no wrinkles, no creases
  • Bathrooms spotlessly clean — fresh towels folded and arranged
  • All personal items, cleaning supplies, and maintenance equipment removed
  • Curtains and blinds open for natural light (we will adjust as needed)
  • Any burnt-out lights replaced, all lamps functioning
  • Fresh flowers or a small welcome arrangement if available

Restaurant Preparation

  • Tables set with best tableware, glasses, and napkin arrangements
  • Signature dishes (5–6) prepared and plated by the chef for the food shoot
  • Bar area stocked and styled (bottles facing forward, glasses arranged)
  • Menu cards or display items placed if part of the regular setting
  • Floor and seating area clean, chairs aligned

General Property

  • Gardens trimmed and watered (ideally the day before the shoot)
  • Walkways, corridors, and common areas clean and clutter-free
  • Any ongoing construction or maintenance paused during shoot hours if possible
  • Pool area clean, water clear (if photographing the pool)
  • Event lawns mowed and cleared for wide-angle shots
  • Historical artefacts, paintings, and decor items dusted and well-placed
  • Any vehicles or parked cars removed from exterior photo areas

Coordination

  • One point-of-contact from the palace available on both shoot days
  • Access to all areas including rooftop/terrace for elevated shots
  • Kitchen team available from 2:00–4:30 PM on Day 1 for food preparation
  • Staff availability if lifestyle/service shots are included

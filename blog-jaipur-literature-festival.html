The Jaipur Literature Festival — and the Palace Where It Began

15 January 2026 · The Diggi Journal

Every January, Jaipur becomes the meeting point for one of the largest literary gatherings in the world. The Jaipur Literature Festival, founded in 2006, has grown into a five-day event drawing authors, poets and readers from across the globe — and its earliest editions were held in the courtyards of Diggi Palace, which remained its home ground for many years.

A festival that started small

The first editions of JLF were modest by later standards — a handful of sessions, a few hundred attendees, held within the palace's gardens and durbar spaces. What made Diggi Palace suited to the festival wasn't scale; it was character. A working heritage palace, with its courtyards, verandas and gardens, offered exactly the kind of informal, layered space that a literature festival needs — somewhere a panel discussion can happen under a tree in the morning and a quiet conversation can continue over chai in a courtyard corner in the afternoon.

Why the setting mattered

Palaces like Diggi were built for exactly this kind of gathering — Rajasthani noble residences were designed with multiple courtyards precisely so that different kinds of company (family, guests, dignitaries, performers) could be hosted simultaneously without crowding into a single hall. That architectural logic, built for 19th-century royal hospitality, turned out to map almost perfectly onto the needs of a modern literary festival: multiple simultaneous sessions, informal mingling space, and a setting distinctive enough to become part of the festival's identity.

A living connection, not a plaque

Many hotels claim historical significance through a single event or a name-drop. Diggi Palace's connection to JLF is different — it's part of the property's living history, referenced by returning guests, festival veterans and Jaipur locals alike. For travellers interested in India's literary and cultural life, staying somewhere that is not just near the festival's story but part of it, is a different kind of experience than reading about it afterward.

Visiting outside festival season

You don't need to time your trip to January to feel this history. The same courtyards that hosted early festival sessions are part of daily palace life year-round — quiet in the mornings, warm in the evenings, and always a reminder that this is a palace that has hosted ideas as readily as it has hosted royalty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where was the Jaipur Literature Festival first held? expand_more

The Jaipur Literature Festival was founded in 2006 and held its earliest editions in the courtyards and gardens of Diggi Palace, Jaipur, which remained its home ground for many years.

Is Diggi Palace still connected to the Jaipur Literature Festival? expand_more

Diggi Palace's history as JLF's founding venue remains part of the property's identity and story, referenced by returning guests and festival veterans, even as the festival itself has grown and its logistics evolved over the years.

Can I visit Diggi Palace outside of festival season? expand_more

Yes — Diggi Palace operates as a heritage hotel year-round, and the same courtyards and gardens associated with the festival's early years are part of everyday palace life outside of January.

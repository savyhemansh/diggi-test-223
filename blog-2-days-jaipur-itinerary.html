2 Days in Jaipur: The Perfect Itinerary from C-Scheme

29 January 2026 · The Diggi Journal

Two days is enough to see Jaipur's essential sights without rushing, provided you plan the route rather than bouncing between neighbourhoods. This itinerary is timed from a C-Scheme base — Diggi Palace's neighbourhood — which sits conveniently between the old walled city and the newer parts of town.

Day 1 morning: Amber Fort

Start early. Amber Fort, roughly 30–40 minutes from C-Scheme by road, is best experienced before the mid-morning tour groups arrive. Give yourself two to three hours to walk the courtyards, the Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace), and the fort's ramparts looking down over Maota Lake.

Day 1 afternoon: City Palace and Jantar Mantar

Head back into the city for the City Palace complex, still partly a royal residence, and the adjoining Jantar Mantar observatory — a UNESCO World Heritage Site of 18th-century astronomical instruments. Both sit within walking distance of each other in the old walled city.

Day 1 evening: Hawa Mahal and the bazaars

Hawa Mahal's honeycomb facade is best photographed in late-afternoon light from the street or a rooftop café opposite. Afterward, wander into Johari Bazaar or Bapu Bazaar for jewellery, textiles and leather goods before heading back to C-Scheme for dinner.

Day 2 morning: heritage and gardens

Spend the morning slower — a walk through a heritage property's gardens and courtyards, like those at Diggi Palace, gives a sense of Rajput residential architecture that the fort-and-palace sightseeing circuit doesn't always show. It's also a good time to arrange a driver for the afternoon's slightly further destinations.

Day 2 afternoon: Nahargarh and Albert Hall

Nahargarh Fort, perched on the Aravalli hills above the city, offers the best panoramic view of Jaipur — worth the drive up for sunset. On the way back, the Albert Hall Museum in Ram Niwas Garden is a good stop for Rajasthani art and artefacts if time allows.

Day 2 evening: rooftop dinner

Close the trip with a rooftop dinner overlooking the old city skyline, or a quieter courtyard meal back at your hotel — Rajasthani thali is the meal to have at least once before you leave.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you see Jaipur properly in 2 days? expand_more

Yes — two days is enough to cover Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal, the old city bazaars and Nahargarh Fort, provided the route is planned by neighbourhood rather than visited randomly.

How far is Amber Fort from C-Scheme? expand_more

Amber Fort is roughly 30–40 minutes from C-Scheme by road, making it an easy early-morning trip before the midday crowds and heat build up.

What should I eat on a short trip to Jaipur? expand_more

A Rajasthani thali — typically including laal maas, dal baati churma and gatte ki sabzi — is the essential meal to have at least once during a Jaipur visit.
